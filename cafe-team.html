The person who started the dream

Meet Abir.

A BTech CSE graduate with a love of data, a freelance journey, and a childhood wish to build a small business of his own. Nexovetra is his idea of a place where people can pause, feel welcome, and make memories over a cup.

The dream also reaches beyond the café: to support his family, create work for others, and one day give back to an old-age home. Every thoughtful visit helps this little idea take a step forward.
