Roles we’re building.

These are draft role listings for the café concept. Email to confirm current availability, schedule, and compensation before applying.

Barista / Coffee Apprentice

Training welcome

Prepare espresso and tea drinks with care, keep the bar clean, and help guests find a drink they’ll love. We can teach recipes; curiosity and kindness matter most.

You’ll do

  • Prepare drinks to house recipes
  • Keep equipment and the counter tidy
  • Welcome guests and explain the menu

You bring

  • Reliable communication and punctuality
  • Interest in coffee and learning
  • Previous café work is helpful, not required

Location: Zaveri Bazaar, Mumbai · Shift details shared during hiring.

Café Service Associate

Guest experience

Help create the easy, welcoming feeling Nexovetra is known for—from greeting guests and taking orders to keeping tables comfortable and ready.

You’ll do

  • Welcome guests and answer menu questions
  • Coordinate orders with the bar and kitchen
  • Keep dining and pickup areas inviting

You bring

  • A friendly, patient manner
  • Comfort working as part of a team
  • Hospitality experience is a plus

Location: Zaveri Bazaar, Mumbai · Shift details shared during hiring.

Kitchen Assistant

Food preparation

Support prep and service for a café menu of vegetarian and non-vegetarian snacks, sandwiches, noodles, and baked treats.

You’ll do

  • Prepare ingredients and assemble dishes
  • Follow food-safety and cleaning routines
  • Coordinate timing with the service team

You bring

  • Care for cleanliness and consistency
  • Ability to work calmly during busy periods
  • Kitchen experience is welcome

Location: Zaveri Bazaar, Mumbai · Shift details shared during hiring.

Shift Lead

Experience preferred

Help the café run smoothly during a shift—support the crew, keep service thoughtful, and make sure small problems are handled with care.

You’ll do

  • Coordinate opening, handover, and closing tasks
  • Support stock checks and daily checklists
  • Coach teammates through busy service

You bring

  • Some hospitality or retail experience
  • Clear communication and steady judgment
  • Interest in helping a small team grow

Location: Zaveri Bazaar, Mumbai · Shift details shared during hiring.
