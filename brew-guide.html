A little coffee know-how

Brew a better
everyday cup.

Good brewing doesn’t need to be complicated. Start with fresh coffee, clean water, and a recipe you can adjust to your taste.

🌤️

01 · Pour-over

Bright & clear

A good way to notice the coffee’s lighter, fruitier notes.

Starter ratio
15 g coffee : 250 ml water
Grind
Medium, like table salt
  1. Rinse the paper filter and warm the brewer.
  2. Add coffee and wet it with a small first pour.
  3. Pour the rest slowly in circles; aim for about 2½–3 minutes total.
🫖

02 · French press

Round & cozy

An easy, full-bodied cup that’s lovely for a slow morning.

Starter ratio
15 g coffee : 250 ml water
Grind
Coarse, like sea salt
  1. Add coffee and hot water, then stir gently.
  2. Place the lid on and steep for about 4 minutes.
  3. Press slowly and pour right away to keep the flavor balanced.
🧊

03 · Cold brew

Smooth & mellow

A patient, chilled brew for warm afternoons. This makes a concentrate to dilute to taste.

Starter ratio
1 part coffee : 8 parts water
Steep
12–16 hours in the fridge
  1. Combine coarse coffee and cool water in a jar.
  2. Cover and chill, then strain through a fine filter.
  3. Serve over ice; add water or milk until it tastes right.
⚖️

04 · Make it yours

Tiny adjustments

These are starting points, not strict rules. Change one thing at a time and keep a note of the cup you enjoy most.

  • Too bitter? Try a coarser grind or a slightly shorter brew.
  • Too sharp or thin? Try a finer grind or a little more coffee.
  • Water matters. Use fresh, clean-tasting water and avoid boiling water directly onto delicate grounds.

Ratios and times are starter recipes. Coffee, grinder, water, and personal taste all change the result.
