✦Dream in progress

A note from Abir

“I want to build a place that feels like a little more is possible.”

Not just a business with my name on it—a warm place for people, a future for my family, and, when I’m able, a way to give a little back.

♥Family firstOne step at a time

A hope for the future — not a promise already fulfilled.

  1. 01

    The first spark

    A shop full of everyday stories.

    As a child, I often went with my father to his grocery shop. I saw how a small business could become part of people’s daily lives—familiar faces, honest work, and conversations across the counter. I also loved the feeling of cafés. Little by little, I began to imagine a place of my own.

    A childhood idea, kept close
  2. 02

    Learning my way

    A curious mind, drawn to data.

    I studied computer science, completed my B.Tech in CSE, and after college started looking for work and taking on freelance projects. Data became something I truly loved—finding patterns, asking better questions, and turning information into something useful.

  3. 03

    The dream takes shape

    Small steps can carry a big hope.

    I come from a lower-middle-class family, so I know a dream has to grow alongside real responsibilities. I’m working toward a café built with patience and care—a place where people can slow down, share a cup, and feel welcome exactly as they are.

  4. 04

    What success means to me

    A future with room to share.

    I hope Nexovetra will help me support my family. And when the business is strong enough, I’d love to share some of its success with an old-age home. That’s the bigger purpose behind the dream, and I’m building toward it one step at a time.

    ♡ For family. For community. For what comes next.

If you’re here, you’re part of the early chapter. Thank you.
