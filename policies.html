Policies & help.

These are starter policies for the Nexovetra website and café concept. Menu prices, order methods, delivery, refunds, and response times should be confirmed by the café before public launch.

Draft prepared 24 November 2026 · Last reviewed: 24 November 2026

01 · Website use

Terms & conditions

Menu and availability

Menu photos, descriptions, and prices on this demo site are examples and can change. Please confirm ingredients, availability, and the final price with the café before placing an order.

Orders and reservations

The order and booking pages prepare a WhatsApp message. You must review and send the message yourself. A request is not confirmed until the café replies and confirms availability.

Food and allergies

Recipes and preparation areas may involve common allergens. Tell the café about allergies before ordering; the team should confirm whether a dish can be prepared safely.

Contact

Questions about an order or visit? Email abirsheepanskura004@gmail.com.

02 · Your information

Privacy policy

The Visit Us contact form asks for your name, age, gender, phone number, country calling code, and Gmail address. This draft site uses those details to receive and reply to enquiries. Please do not use the form to send passwords, payment-card numbers, or other sensitive information.

When the site is run through its Node server, the server forwards form submissions to FormSubmit for email delivery and does not save the submission to a local database. FormSubmit says submissions may be retained for 30 days; see FormSubmit’s privacy information ↗ for its current handling details.

Some page styling and photos load from third-party services, which may receive standard connection information from your browser. For questions or to ask us to remove an enquiry from our mailbox, email abirsheepanskura004@gmail.com. Requests about information held by FormSubmit may also need to be made to that service.

03 · Takeaway

Shipping & pickup

The current website supports takeaway requests prepared in WhatsApp for pickup from Nexovetra Café in Zaveri Bazaar, Kalbadevi, Mumbai 400002. It does not take online payment or arrange courier shipping.

Choose a pickup time in the order page, review the message, and send it in WhatsApp. Wait for the café to confirm the order and timing before travelling. Any delivery availability, fee, or estimated time should be confirmed directly with the café.

04 · If something went wrong

Returns & refunds

This website does not currently accept online payments, so it does not issue website refunds. If a café order is incorrect or has a quality issue, please tell the team as soon as possible and share the order details so they can review what happened.

Any remedy for an in-person purchase should be discussed directly with the café. The final refund and replacement process needs to be confirmed by the business before this draft is published as a final policy.

Email us about an order ↗

05 · We’re listening

Grievance redressal

For a complaint, feedback, or concern about a visit, order, or website enquiry, email abirsheepanskura004@gmail.com with a short description and, if relevant, the visit date or order reference.

We’ll review the details and reply by email. A named grievance contact and response timeline should be added here by the café before launch.
